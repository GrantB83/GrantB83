# GuestFlow Lead Actions - Staff Quick Start Guide

## What's New?

You can now respond to guest inquiries faster with one-click actions! 🎉

## How to Use

### 1. Open the CRM

Visit: **http://localhost:3100/crm** (or your production URL)

You'll see a list of all guest inquiries.

### 2. Click on a Lead

Click on any row to open the lead details page.

### 3. Use Quick Actions

Four buttons at the top help you respond quickly:

#### 📋 Copy WEBDIRECT Link
- **What it does:** Copies the booking link to your clipboard
- **When to use:** When replying to a guest via email or WhatsApp
- **What happens:** Button changes to "Copied!" - now paste it anywhere

The link is: `https://book.nightsbridge.com/24299?promocode=WEBDIRECT`

#### 🏠 Open Nightsbridge
- **What it does:** Opens the Nightsbridge dashboard in a new tab
- **When to use:** When you need to check availability or booking calendar
- **What happens:** New browser tab opens with Nightsbridge login

#### 💬 Draft WhatsApp
- **What it does:** Creates a pre-written WhatsApp message with guest's name, dates, and booking link
- **When to use:** When responding to inquiry via WhatsApp
- **What happens:** 
  1. A draft message appears on screen
  2. Click "Copy Draft" to copy it
  3. Open WhatsApp on your phone/computer
  4. Paste the message
  5. **Review it before sending!**

**Important:** Always review the message before sending. Never send automatically!

#### 📊 Status Dropdown
- **What it does:** Updates the lead's status to track progress
- **When to use:** As you work through the inquiry
- **Statuses:**
  - **New** - Just received, not contacted yet
  - **Contacted** - You've replied to the guest
  - **Qualified** - Guest confirmed interest and dates
  - **Won** - Booking confirmed!
  - **Lost** - Guest went elsewhere
  - **Converted** - Guest booked via WEBDIRECT link

### 4. Add Notes (Optional but Helpful!)

At the bottom of the page:
1. Type a note about this lead (e.g., "WhatsApp sent, guest wants breakfast info")
2. Click "Save Note"
3. The note appears with a timestamp

**Why add notes?**
- Track what you've done
- Help other staff members
- Remember special requests
- Follow up later

## Example Workflow

**Scenario:** Sarah Miller submitted an inquiry for Dec 15-17

1. Go to **/crm**
2. Click on "Sarah Miller" row
3. See her inquiry message: "Looking for a romantic getaway..."
4. Click **"Draft WhatsApp"**
5. Review the generated message (has her name, dates, and link)
6. Click **"Copy Draft"**
7. Open WhatsApp
8. Paste and send (after reviewing!)
9. Go back to GuestFlow
10. Change status to **"Contacted"**
11. Add note: "WhatsApp sent, awaiting response"
12. Done! ✅

## Common Questions

### Q: Can I edit the WhatsApp message before sending?
**A:** Yes! You should always review and personalize it. The draft is just a starting point.

### Q: What if the guest hasn't provided dates?
**A:** The WhatsApp draft will still work - it just won't include specific dates. You can add them manually if you discussed them later.

### Q: Can I send the WhatsApp message automatically?
**A:** No - you must always copy, review, and send manually. This ensures you can personalize each message.

### Q: What's the difference between "Won" and "Converted"?
**A:** 
- **Won** = Booking is confirmed (you agreed on dates and price)
- **Converted** = Guest actually completed booking through WEBDIRECT link

### Q: Can I use this for email replies too?
**A:** Yes! Just use the "Copy WEBDIRECT Link" button and paste it into your email.
