Tesla - Operations Manager, Gigafactory

Location: Austin, TX (Hybrid)

About the Role:
Tesla is seeking an experienced Operations Manager to lead our Gigafactory operations team. You will be responsible for managing production operations, implementing lean manufacturing principles, and driving continuous improvement initiatives.

Responsibilities:
- Manage daily operations of production teams
- Implement and maintain lean manufacturing processes
- Lead cross-functional teams to optimize production efficiency
- Manage operational budgets and resources
- Ensure safety protocols and compliance
- Coordinate with engineering teams on product launches
- Drive continuous improvement using Six Sigma methodologies

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Operations Management, Industrial Engineering, or related field
- 5+ years of operations management experience in automotive or manufacturing
- Strong knowledge of lean manufacturing and Six Sigma
- Experience with ERP systems (SAP preferred)
- Proven track record of cost reduction and process optimization
- Excellent leadership and communication skills

Compensation: $120,000 - $160,000 annually plus benefits and stock options

Tesla is an Equal Opportunity Employer.
